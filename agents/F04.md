TC04 covers the supplied fresh-account case: login on the same car page exposes an enabled
Vote! control before any manual refresh. It does not exercise an already-voted account.
TC08 also checks comment-input removal; that is an additional guest-state assertion,
not a replacement for the spreadsheet's attempted-vote step.

These are **coverage differences**, not automatically product defects or automation failures. A UI that removes the control may make an attempted second click impossible.
